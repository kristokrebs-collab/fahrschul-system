# Audience — The Invisible Why

## Who this channel is for

- English-speaking, primarily US / UK / CA / AU
- Age 20–40, above-average curiosity, above-average disposable income trajectory
- Watches Veritasium, Kurzgesagt, Vox, ColdFusion, Half as Interesting, PolyMatter,
  Practical Engineering — but wants shorter, punchier, more personally-relevant
  hooks than pure science explainers
- Cares about money, psychology, health, and "systems that are quietly working
  on me" — not abstract trivia
- Watches YouTube on TV or phone in the evening, often multiple videos in a row
  (sessions matter — end screens and related-topic chaining matter)

## Who this channel is explicitly NOT for

- Kids' content / family-safe-only advertisers (we can go a little edgy)
- Pure entertainment / meme audiences with no interest in "why"
- Hardcore academic viewers who want citations mid-sentence (we cite in
  description + pinned comment, not by interrupting the story)

## The viewer's implicit question

Every video answers a version of: **"What is actually going on here, and why
did nobody tell me?"** The viewer should finish a video slightly more
suspicious of their environment (casinos, supermarkets, apps, their own
brain) in a fun way, not a paranoid way.

## What makes a viewer subscribe

1. The video answered a question they didn't know they had
2. The explanation felt earned (evidence, not assertion)
3. The channel has a recognizable voice + visual identity across videos
4. The ending pays off — a twist, a reframe, or a practical takeaway

## What makes a viewer leave mid-video

- Slow cold open / channel intro / "before we start, subscribe"
- Restating the title for 60 seconds before adding new information
- Visual monotony (nothing changes on screen for >40s)
- Hedge-everything language that removes all narrative tension
