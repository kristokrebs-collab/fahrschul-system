## Recommended pairing

Rationale: The title states the exact promise the video delivers (three named
biases + the dark-pattern research), and the thumbnail is legible at
small sizes with a single clear conflict. It avoids over-promising a
"secret" the video doesn't have — the video literally names and explains
the mechanisms, so there's no gap between click and payoff.

research — the payoff beat (8:35–9:50) explicitly answers the title's
claim. Risk is only that "refuses" slightly personifies the brain for
punch; script never overstates this into a false certainty (dossier
caveats around the FTC rule status and the fairness point at 6:00 keep it
honest).
