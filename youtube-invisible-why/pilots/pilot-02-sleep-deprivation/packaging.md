## Recommended pairing
Title: **What 36 Hours Without Sleep Does to Your Decisions**
Thumbnail: Concept 2 — dial with needle in the red zone, text "LEGALLY IMPAIRED"
Rationale: "36 hours" and "decisions" both appear in the title, matching
the thumbnail's dial metaphor without repeating the title's words on the
image itself. The BAC-equivalence finding (Dawson & Reid 1997; Williamson
& Feyer 2000) is the strongest, most surprising, most defensible hook in
the dossier — leading with it in the thumbnail is honest, not just
attention-grabbing, because the video spends real time substantiating it.

## Clickbait risk assessment
Score: **2/5**. "Legally impaired" is a strong claim, but it's directly
sourced (Finding 1 in the research dossier) and explicitly explained
on-screen with the actual comparison studies, not left as an unsupported
assertion. The one risk to manage: the thumbnail must not imply sleep loss
literally *is* alcohol intoxication (a false-equivalence risk) — the
script's phrasing ("performed about as well as," never "is the same as")
and the dossier's explicit caveat under Finding 1 keep this from
overclaiming.
