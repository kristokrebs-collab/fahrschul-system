## Description
```
You walked in for milk. You left with milk and seven other things. That
wasn't weak willpower — it was a space that has been measured, mapped,
and adjusted long before you pushed the cart through the door.

This video shows the actual structure underneath a store you've walked
through a thousand times: the decompression zone at the entrance, why the
milk is never near the door, what researchers found when they tracked
real shoppers' walking paths with in-store location data, and why shelf
height alone measurably changes what sells.

It also covers the part most videos skip: not all of it is manipulation.
Refrigeration and delivery logistics are real constraints too — the
interesting question is where the line sits.
```
